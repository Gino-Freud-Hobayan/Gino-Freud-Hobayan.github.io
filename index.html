About me

I'm a tech professional working across data analysis, software development, and game development. I've built projects ranging from large-scale SQL analytics to a solo Unity RPG, and I'm currently exploring where these skills can take me next.

Before tech, I was a consistent academic scholar who took up a career in Marine Transportation, where I got licensed as a Merchant Marine Officer and worked onboard oil-chemical tankers as a Seafarer for almost 3 years.
When the COVID-19 pandemic put that career on hold, I adapted and picked up new skills, working in the Financial Services industry and Sales for almost 2 years before moving into tech.
Since then, I've completed several capstone projects, studied Mathematics, finished the Google Data Analytics Professional Certificate, and completed a Data Science and Business Analytics Internship at The Sparks Foundation.
I've also been building a strong foundation in software development, with a particular focus on game development. I'm currently developing Survivor's Mask, a solo 2D action RPG in Unity, as I continue exploring where my skills in data, software, and game development can take me.
Outside of work, I stay active through weightlifting and running, since I believe there's a strong positive correlation between physical and mental health.
